# 🚀 Favorite Movie/Book: Project Hail Mary

> *"Fist my bump!"* — Rocky

---

### 📌 Overview
**Project Hail Mary** is an incredible sci-fi story written by **Andy Weir** (author of *The Martian*) and adapted into a major feature film starring **Ryan Gosling**. 

### 🌌 Plot Summary
* **Protagonist:** Dr. Ryland Grace, a middle school science teacher turned reluctant astronaut.
* **The Mission:** Earth is facing an extinction-level event caused by an energy-eating microbe called `Astrophage`. Grace is sent to the Tau Ceti solar system to find a cure.
* **The Twist:** Grace wakes up from a coma with amnesia, surrounded by his deceased crewmates, and must piece together who he is and why he is there. Along the way, he encounters an unexpected alien ally named **Rocky**!

### 💡 Why I Love It
- **Real Science & Problem Solving:** It uses real-world physics, chemistry, and biology to solve space survival challenges.
- **Unforgettable Friendship:** The dynamic between Ryland Grace and Rocky shows the power of communication and teamwork across species.
